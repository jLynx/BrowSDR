About IQ recordings

IQ files contain the radio samples across the full received band, before audio or decoding. Use compatible signal analysis software to inspect, demodulate, or decode them. Files use SigMF with signed 8-bit or 16-bit I/Q, or normalized 32-bit float. 12-bit samples use 16-bit storage. Choosing 8-bit halves the file size compared with 16-bit and discards fine sample detail.

Stop recording saves the samples captured so far. Cancel recording discards them. Keep this tab open. Retuning, changing gains, or stopping reception cancels the recording.
