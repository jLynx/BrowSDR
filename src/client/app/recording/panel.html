About IQ recordings

IQ files contain the radio samples across the full received band, before audio or decoding. Use compatible signal analysis software to inspect, demodulate, or decode them. Files use SigMF format with signed 8-bit I/Q samples.

Keep this tab open. Retuning, changing gains, or stopping reception cancels the recording. Incomplete recordings are discarded.
