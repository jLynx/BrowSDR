Radio counters cover this VFO. Correction details below are for the selected base.

Radio frames require CRC16/MODBUS; correction containers also require RTCM CRC24Q. Beacons carry no corrections. Sequence gaps are observed counter increments, not a measurement of USB loss.

RTK2 packets require CRC8; the container format also requires CRC16/MODBUS. Compact packets carry raw correction bytes. Corrections require RTCM CRC24Q. Compact packets have no verified sequence counter; sequence gaps describe the container format. Keepalives carry no corrections. Packets missed before header detection do not increase checksum errors; missing fragments can delay complete corrections.
