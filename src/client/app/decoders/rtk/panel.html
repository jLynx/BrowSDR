Decoder settings

RTK3 support follows the published RBS03 protocol and has not been tested with hardware. Tune the VFO to an active channel in the waterfall. Hopping bases send intermittent bursts on each channel; automatic hop following is not available.

RTK2 can change channel during startup. If this preset is quiet, tune to its active signal in the waterfall.

Measured presets: 20 for RTK1, 10 for RTK2. RTK3 starts at 0; adjust for your receiver.
