Decoder settings

RTK3 support follows the published RBS03 protocol and has not been tested with hardware. Tune the VFO to an active channel in the waterfall. Hopping bases send intermittent bursts on each channel; automatic hop following is not available.

RTK2 can change channel during startup. If this preset is quiet, tune to its active signal in the waterfall.

Measured presets: 20 for RTK1, 10 for RTK2. RTK3 starts at 0; adjust for your receiver.

A strong waterfall signal can still lose packets through interference or sample timing mismatch. Timing presets are not calibrated for every SDR; GNSS signal quality describes the satellites at the base, not this radio link.

Radio frames require CRC16/MODBUS; correction containers also require RTCM CRC24Q. Beacons carry no corrections. Sequence gaps are observed counter increments, not a measurement of USB loss.

RTK2 packets require CRC8 and container CRC16/MODBUS; corrections also require RTCM CRC24Q. Keepalives carry no corrections. Sequence gaps count skipped base packet numbers, not USB loss.
